Primary Lithium Battery SOC Prediction System for Estimating Life-time of Industrial IoT Devices  
**Minji Kang**, Seongseop Kim, Seungwoo Lee  
Autumn Annual Conference of The institute of Electronics and Information Engineers, 2023  
2023-11-24
<details>
  <summary>Abstract</summary>
  <div markdown="1">
    In this paper, we propose a method for estimating lithium primary battery capacity in embedded device systems. The proposed technology is a method of predicting remaining power using the voltage characteristics of lithium primary batteries and profile-based operation modes. In order to apply the proposed remaining capacity prediction structure to embedded systems, the parameters using LUT are designed to be lightweight. As a result, it was confirmed that the accuracy of estimating the remaining primary battery capacity of the proposed module was with 10%.

      - title: Analysis of EDLC-based Primary Battery Current Consumption Characteristics Considering Operation Patterns in IoT Devices
        authors:
          - Minji Kang, Seongseop Kim, Seungwoo Lee
        publisher: The 10th International Conference on Electronics, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science 2022
        releaseDate: 2022-07-21
        url: 
        summary: This paper presents an analysis of the discharge characteristics of EDLC-based primary battery in accordance with the operating characteristics of IoT devices. To extract discharge characteristics, we analyzed the difference between an EDLC-based primary battery and a primary battery not using EDLC after the experiment. The current consumption pattern of the IoT device used in the experiment has a structure that includes IDLE, Processing, and communication transmission/reception, and we comparatively analyzed the voltage change at this time. As a result, it shows that the voltage recovery time of the EDLC-based primary battery is up to 15 times greater than the regular one, but the average of the maximum voltage regulation is about 11 times less.

      - title: Battery Lifetime Estimation System for IoT Devices using Voltage Measurement
        authors:
          - Seongseop Kim, Minji Kang, Youngmin Kwon, Seungwoo Lee
        publishers: Institute of Embedded Engineering of Korea 2021
        releaseDate: 2021-11-11
        summary: Primary battery has been widely used in many industry, because of its advantage in high capacity and low self discharging. However, due to the characteristic of a sudden drop in voltage before replacing the battery, it is hard to replace the battery before discharging. In this study, we propose a measurement technique for battery lifetime estimation based on voltage recovery measurement. The voltage recovery time of a primary lithium battery was measured using electronic load, which can load specific current, same as embedded system. The result shows that the voltage recovery time of the primary lithium battery became slower as battery capacity decreased, which can be implemented in embedded system.
